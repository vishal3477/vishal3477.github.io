**Can a compact printed antenna support two separated operating bands?** This earlier research studies how patch rotation and an additional feed-connected patch change impedance matching.

### How it works

- Use a **20 x 25 x 1.6 mm** structure on an FR4 substrate.
- Combine an elliptical slot and rotated patch with a secondary patch connected to the microstrip feed.
- Shape the partial ground plane and study the effect of geometric parameters in CST simulation.

### A reported result

> **Simulated impedance bands of 2.5-4.3 GHz and 8.1-15 GHz**, using the **S11 < -10 dB** criterion reported in the abstract.

These are impedance-matching ranges, not data rates or a guarantee of equal gain throughout each band. The parameter study examines how rotation and secondary-patch size change the response.

### Scope

The headline ranges above are **simulation results**. They should not be presented as measured over-the-air communication performance, product certification, or evidence of performance outside the reported substrate and geometry.
