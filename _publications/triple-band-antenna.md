**How much multiband behavior can fit into a small printed antenna?** This earlier work combines feed and patch modifications to target Wi-Fi, WiMAX, and an X-band frequency range.

### How it works

- Use a **15 x 20 x 1.6 mm** FR4 structure.
- Combine an extended microstrip feed with an I-shaped patch containing a rectangular slot.
- Add a partial ground plane with symmetric parasitic elements and evaluate the geometric design through simulation and measurement.

### A reported result

> **Simulated impedance bands of 2.34-2.46 GHz, 4.61-5.92 GHz, and 9-9.9 GHz**, under the **S11 < -10 dB** criterion, as reported in the abstract.

The paper also compares simulated and measured return loss in **Figure 2**. The ranges quoted here are the simulation values, not a replacement for the measured curve.

### Scope

Compactness comes with a trade-off: the paper reports low gain in the lowest-frequency band. Impedance matching alone does not establish uniform radiation performance or certify a complete wireless product.
