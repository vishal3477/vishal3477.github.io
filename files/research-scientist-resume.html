Research Profile

Research Scientist at Adobe with a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering, specializing in trustworthy generative AI, media provenance, and proactive image forensics. Develops watermarking and attribution methods to trace creative influences in generated content and detect image manipulation. Research spans diffusion models, model reverse engineering, and video watermarking, with publications at CVPR, ICCV, NeurIPS, TPAMI, and IJCV.

Research & Technical Expertise

Research: Generative AI attribution; image, video, and audio watermarking; deepfake detection; manipulation localization; diffusion-model customization; robustness evaluation; C2PA and Content Credentials.

Methods: Proactive learning, textual inversion, token embeddings, encoder-decoder architectures, vision-language models, low-rank adaptation (LoRA).

Implementation: Python, PyTorch, PyTorch Lightning, TensorFlow, NumPy, scikit-learn, OpenCV, CUDA, C/C++, SQL; AWS and Google Cloud Storage.
